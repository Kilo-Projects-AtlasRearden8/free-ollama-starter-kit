How to use this site

  1. Start with Ollama: it's the engine that runs local AI models on your computer.
  2. Pick a model: Llama 3.2 (3B) is a good first choice for most laptops.
  3. Add a frontend (optional): Open WebUI, AnythingLLM, or LM Studio give you a chat window instead of the terminal.
  4. Collect prompts: download a prompt collection and paste prompts into your local chat — fully offline.
